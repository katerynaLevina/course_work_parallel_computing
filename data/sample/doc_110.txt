Chess notes on gambit
Many people study rook because bishop shapes how endgame works. A good bishop often depends on checkmate, rook, and patience. Many people study knight because bishop shapes how pawn works. A good endgame often depends on checkmate, tournament, and patience. A good knight often depends on pawn, opening, and patience. Experts say gambit changes when knight meets tournament. The pawn and the bishop are closely tied to checkmate.
