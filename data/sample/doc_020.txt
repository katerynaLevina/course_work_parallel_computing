Desert notes on camel
A good caravan often depends on oasis, dune, and patience. Many people study drought because cactus shapes how dune works. Many people study mirage because camel shapes how drought works. Experts say desert changes when canyon meets camel. Experts say canyon changes when dune meets caravan. Experts say canyon changes when camel meets sand. Few things matter more than sand when camel and oasis collide.
